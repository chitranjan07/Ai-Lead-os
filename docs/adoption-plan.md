# First Two Weeks Adoption Plan

## Week 1 — Observe

Instrument workflow events: leads reviewed, analyses run, approved/edited/rejected drafts, manual-review reasons, send failures, follow-up date changes, and time-to-action. Collect counselor feedback using anonymized examples.

Success signals:
- lower average time per follow-up
- fewer manual navigation/touches
- fewer missed/late follow-up tasks
- high acceptance rate without suppressing needed edits

## Week 2 — Learn and tune

Add newly observed failures to the evaluation set. Review false-positive priorities, wrong stages, wrong dates, channel mistakes, message edits, and safety/manual-review cases. Tune prompts/rules and rerun regression before changing production behavior.

## Guardrails

Do not expand to autonomous external messaging until accuracy, opt-out handling, provider status handling, and organizational approval requirements have been validated with real users.
